# ENGINEERING CONSTITUTION & OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLES
## FOOTBALL AI INTELLIGENCE & MACHINE-LEARNING PLATFORM

### ARTICLE I — REAL DATA & ZERO-FABRICATION PRINCIPLE
1. **Zero Fake Football Data**: Under no circumstances shall production code, inference pipelines, or reporting modules fabricate match results, goals, xG, shots, cards, injuries, lineups, fixtures, or probabilities.
2. **Explicit Nullity**: When data is absent or unavailable from verified sources, it MUST be explicitly represented as `NULL` / `UNAVAILABLE` and handled upstream by feature extractors and abstention rules.
3. **Isolated Testing Data**: Synthetic data, test fixtures, or mocks are strictly restricted to unit/integration testing environments in `tests/` and MUST be explicitly flagged (e.g., `is_test_fixture=True`). Synthetic test data must never leak into production databases or training sets.

---

### ARTICLE II — TIME-AWARE VALIDATION & LEAKAGE PREVENTION
1. **Chronological Splitting**: All dataset splitting for model training, validation, and backtesting MUST strictly respect temporal ordering (`event_timestamp <= prediction_timestamp`). Random k-fold cross-validation across time boundaries is forbidden.
2. **Pre-Match Feature Availability**: Features engineered for match forecast $M_t$ at cutoff time $t$ MUST ONLY ingest information generated at or before $t$. Any feature relying on post-kickoff data or post-event statistics is a severe architectural defect.

---

### ARTICLE III — ENTITY CANONICALIZATION & FEATURE SEPARATION
1. **Canonical Identifiers**: All clubs, competitions, players, and venues MUST be mapped to immutable canonical identifiers (e.g., UUIDs / entity IDs) via alias resolution.
2. **Prohibition of Reputation Features**: Popularity, fanbase size, media coverage, prestige, or historical fame MUST NEVER be used as numerical inputs to predictive models. All team strength metrics must be mathematically derived from performance statistics.

---

### ARTICLE IV — ABSTENTION, RISK & NO-BET AS FIRST-CLASS OUTPUT
1. **NO-BET Mandate**: The platform is explicitly designed to decline forecasting when data quality, model uncertainty, lineup instability, or market noise exceed acceptable safety thresholds.
2. **Abstention Criteria**: An output of `NO BET / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` is considered a valid, successful operational outcome.

---

### ARTICLE V — AUDITABILITY & PROVENANCE
1. **Full Traceability**: Every production prediction generated by the platform MUST store an unalterable audit log containing:
   - Match & Entity UUIDs
   - Prediction Timestamp & Cutoff Time
   - Source data provenance URLs & retrieval timestamps
   - Exact numerical features & feature version schema
   - Model ID, algorithm version, and probability calibration version
   - Model confidence, uncertainty metrics, risk score, and final decision (`PREDICTION` vs `NO_BET`)

---

### ARTICLE VI — STAGE-GATED DEVELOPMENT & EXECUTION CONTROL
1. **Sequential Stages**: Development follows Stages 1 through 26 in exact order. Working on future stages before previous stage completion is strictly forbidden.
2. **Completion Standard**: A stage is complete ONLY when it is fully IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, VERIFIED, DOCUMENTED, and all ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA have PASSED.
