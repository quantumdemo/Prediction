## 2. Coverage Findings & Missingness Patterns

1. **Top Tier European Division Tier**:
   * Leagues `E0`, `E1`, `E2`, `E3`, `EC`, `SP1`, `SP2`, `D1`, `D2`, `I1`, `I2`, `F1`, `F2`, `N1`, `B1`, `P1`, `G1`, `T1`, `SCO` contain **100% complete match statistics** (Shots, Corners, Fouls, Yellow Cards, Red Cards).
2. **Secondary & Americas/Asia League Tier**:
   * Non-European or secondary leagues (`ARG`, `BRA`, `USA`, `MEX`, `JAP`, `CHN`, `AUT`, `SUI`, `DEN`, `NOR`, `SWE`, `POL`, `ROU`, `IRL`, `FIN`, `RUS`) contain full match outcomes (`FTHome`, `FTAway`, `FTResult`) and bookmaker odds, but **0% in-match statistics** (`Shots`, `Corners`, `Fouls`, `Cards`).
3. **Implication for Stage 9 Feature Engineering**:
   * Models trained exclusively on match outcome signals (Poisson / Dixon-Coles goal expectation) can run across all 38 divisions.
   * Complex models requiring shot ratios or corner statistics must be restricted to the 19 primary European divisions where statistical coverage is 100%.
