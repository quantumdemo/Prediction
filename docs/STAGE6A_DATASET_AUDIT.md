# Stage 6A Candidate Dataset Audit Report

## Executive Summary

This document presents the complete audit of the candidate historical football dataset `xgabora/club-football-match-data` (spanning 2000 through September 2026). The candidate dataset was acquired, preserved with SHA-256 checksums, and rigorously evaluated against the project's **Master Control Specification**, **Engineering Constitution**, and **Stage 6 Database Baseline**.

The audit confirms that the dataset contains **238,858 match records** across **38 divisions** in **27 countries** and **273,972 historical Elo snapshot records**. While match results, dates, and core statistical metrics (shots, corners, fouls, cards) are verified and suitable for canonical historical ingestion, **pre-calculated features (Form3, Form5, Clusters, Rating)**, **provisional post-June 2025 Elo ratings**, **odds-derived Expected Goals**, and **bookmaker market fields** must be strictly isolated or rejected from production ML model input pipelines.

## 4. Key Audit Findings & Compliance Directives

1. **Strict Rejection of Bookmaker Odds as ML Features**:
   * Columns `OddHome`, `OddDraw`, `OddAway`, `MaxHome`, `MaxDraw`, `MaxAway`, `Over25`, `Under25`, `HandiHome`, `HandiAway`, `HandiLine`, `ImpliedProbHome`, `ImpliedProbDraw`, `ImpliedProbAway` represent betting market prices. Under **Master Control Specification REQ-MKT-004** and **Engineering Constitution Article III**, bookmaker odds are **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** from being used as predictive model inputs.
2. **Rejection of Synthetic "Expected Goals"**:
   * `ExpectedGoalsHome` and `ExpectedGoalsAway` are linear approximations calculated from shot targets and odds rather than true spatial freeze-frame xG feeds (StatsBomb/Opta). Under **Engineering Constitution Article IX**, synthetic metrics MUST NOT be substituted for real spatial xG. Classified as `REJECTED_UNVERIFIED`.
3. **Mandatory Recomputation of Form & Derived Features**:
   * `Form3Home`, `Form5Home`, `Form3Away`, `Form5Away`, `Form3_Diff`, `Form5_Diff`, `ClusterLabel`, and `ClusterProb` are pre-calculated features. To guarantee **zero future-data leakage**, all rolling form metrics must be recomputed deterministically from raw historical match outcomes during **Stage 9 (Feature Engineering Engine)**.
4. **Provisional Post-June 2025 Elo Ratings**:
   * Elo snapshot ratings after June 1, 2025 are author-simulated continuations. They must be tagged as `PROVISIONAL_ESTIMATE` rather than official ClubElo snapshots.
